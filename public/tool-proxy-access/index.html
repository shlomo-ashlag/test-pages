Tool Google Workspace Admins

Proxy access

The work apps open through the QA proxy for the members of one Google group, from the addresses in one access level. This page shows both lists and lets you change them. It talks to Google straight from your browser, with your own admin account.

Sign in

Use a Google Workspace account that may manage the group and the access level. Google asks for two permissions, the page needs both.

You are signed in to Google. The sign-in lasts about an hour.

Proxy addresses

Requests from these addresses pass the access level .

Group members

Members of the group may open the work apps.
